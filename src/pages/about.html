Our History

Our mark in driving academic excellence was stamped 35 years ago with the vision to provide the right environment for optimum growth, development and actualisation of every student who comes through our doors.

Whether it is tuition or daycare centre in Klang, within the Klang Valley, or even beyond, the MyDelta name has come to represent the same ideals and values that placed MyDelta in a league of its own since 1988.

We provide a safe and enriching home away from home filled with countless and constant opportunities to learn, grow and develop. It is a place where the children's emotional needs are met, their interests nurtured, and their individuality respected at all times.

Mission, Vision & Values

We aim to provide engaging classes to stimulate students’ desire to learn. We strive to build a positive environment that brings happiness to the students. We’re committed to cater to the students’ needs to help them embark in a fun learning journey.
