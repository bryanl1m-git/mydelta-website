Our story, our history, and the mission we teach by.

Our Story

Quality education, and the whole child

At MyDelta we are honoured to carry on a legacy of quality education and care for every child placed with us.

We are a team of educators in Klang. With 35 years of experience, we shape the day around each student’s needs, and we want every child to feel valued in the room.

Learning should be engaging. We ask students to question, share ideas, and talk about what they are learning.

Our History

The work started 35 years ago: the right environment for every student who comes through the door.

Whether it is tuition or daycare in Klang, within the Klang Valley, or further out, the MyDelta name has stood for the same ideals since 1988.

It is a safe home away from home, with steady chances to learn and grow. Emotional needs are met, interests are nurtured, and individuality is respected.

Mission, vision, and values

Our Vision

To provide quality and holistic learning, nurturing characters while equipping students with the necessary tools in their pursuit forwards academic excellence.

Our Mission

We aim to provide engaging classes to stimulate students’ desire to learn. We strive to build a positive environment that brings happiness to the students. We’re committed to cater to the students’ needs to help them embark in a fun learning journey.
