Primary Daycare

A school-day centre in Bandar Botanik for Year 1 to Year 6. No pricing on this page.

Care that lets parents work the day

Our staff build a safe, positive room where children can learn and settle. Every child gets individual attention.

We know the juggle of work and family. The centre is a home away from home for families in the community, with meals, tuition, and optional transport built into the day.

Primary Tuition

Tuition is part of the daycare day, in the sessions on the timetable. There is no separate tuition page, and there are no fees listed here.

Subjects: Bahasa Melayu, English, Mandarin, Science, Maths.

Additional for Year 4, 5, 6: Sejarah, Karangan, Essay, 作文.

What sets us apart

Caring and qualified staff

Childcare professionals who want to work with children, in a safe and stimulating room.

Safe and secure environment

Secure entry, monitored surveillance, and strict safety guidelines. Your child’s safety comes first.

Wholesome food

Balanced meals and snacks, prepared with fresh ingredients. Dietary needs and allergies are accommodated.

Open communication

Daily reports, newsletters, and parent-teacher conferences, so you can follow progress and milestones.

Engaging learning

Tutors build a rapport and use activities, multimedia, and real examples so lessons stay relatable.

Transportation

Optional transport between home, school, and MyDelta for families with a full day.
