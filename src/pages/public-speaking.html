What students gain

Communication skills

Clearer speech, voice, and body language, practised out loud.

Critical thinking

Organising a thought and presenting it in an order other people can follow.

Confidence

Speaking to a group, often enough that it carries into class and daily life.

Leadership

Practice at encouraging others, which is where a young leader starts.

Personal growth

Facing nerves and coming back the next week a little steadier.

A wider circle

Time with children from other schools and years, in the same room.

FAQ

What sets MyDelta apart?

Trainers who work with children, in a supportive room, with enough practice time for each speaker.

How do you handle stage fright?

Relaxation, positive reinforcement, and gradual practice somewhere the child feels safe to try.

How do sessions stay engaging?

Games, role-play, and storytelling, matched to primary age, in Bahasa Melayu and English.

How do I enrol my child?

Send a WhatsApp message or use the contact form. We reply Mon–Fri, 8:00 am – 6:00 pm.
